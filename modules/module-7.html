Apps That Help Every Day

Maps, banking, health, shopping, entertainment — your phone or tablet can handle more of daily life than you might realise.

🟡 Intermediate ⏱ 13 minutes 📖 Reading

What You'll Learn

  • What "apps" are and how to find and install them
  • Maps and navigation — never get lost again
  • Online banking — safe, convenient, and available 24/7
  • Health and NHS apps for managing your healthcare
  • Entertainment: YouTube, music, podcasts, and more

What Is an App?

An "app" is short for "application" — a program designed to do a specific job on your phone or tablet. Just like programs on a computer (Word, Excel), apps are programs designed for mobile devices.

Apps are downloaded from an app store — a digital shop run by the device maker:

  • Apple App Store — for iPhones and iPads
  • Google Play Store — for Android phones and tablets

Most apps are completely free. Some have a one-off purchase price, and some offer extra features for a monthly subscription. When you download an app, it installs in seconds and appears as an icon on your device.

💡

Installing an App

Open the App Store (or Play Store), type the name of the app in the search bar, tap the app when it appears, then tap "Get" (Apple) or "Install" (Android). That's it — the app will appear on your home screen in moments.

🗺️ Maps and Navigation

Getting lost is now much harder with maps apps. These apps know exactly where you are (using GPS) and can give you turn-by-turn directions to anywhere — whether you're driving, walking, or taking public transport.

  • Google Maps — the most comprehensive. Shows live traffic, street view photos, business information, and transport options. Free.
  • Apple Maps — built into iPhones and iPads. Works very well and integrates seamlessly.
  • What3Words — a useful app that assigns every 3-metre square of the world a unique combination of three words. Invaluable in emergencies when you need to tell someone exactly where you are.

Using Google Maps

1

Open Google Maps

Tap the Google Maps icon. The map shows your current location as a blue dot.

2

Search for your destination

Tap the search bar at the top. Type an address, a place name, or even just "chemist near me."

3

Choose Directions

Tap "Directions" and choose whether you're walking, driving, or taking transit.

4

Follow the route

Tap "Start" and the app will speak directions aloud as you travel. It automatically recalculates if you go a different way.

🏦 Online Banking

Online banking lets you view your accounts, make payments, transfer money, and check statements — all from home, at any time of day or night. It's safe, convenient, and used by millions of people.

What You Can Do

  • Check your current balance instantly
  • View recent transactions and statements
  • Pay bills and set up direct debits
  • Transfer money to family or businesses
  • Report a lost or stolen card
  • Set up and manage savings goals
🏦

Setting Up Online Banking

Contact your bank directly (via phone, branch, or their official website) to set up online banking. They'll give you secure login details and guide you through the process. All major UK banks — Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, Halifax — have dedicated apps and websites.

⚠️

Always Go Directly to Your Bank

Only access your bank through its official app (downloaded from the App Store) or by typing its address directly into your browser. Never follow links from emails or texts claiming to be from your bank — this is the most common banking scam.

🏥 Health and NHS Apps

Managing your healthcare is much easier with the right apps:

  • NHS App — book GP appointments, order repeat prescriptions, view your health records, and access your NHS COVID pass. Available free from the App Store or Play Store.
  • Your GP surgery's own app — many surgeries now have their own apps for booking appointments.
  • Pharmacy apps — Boots, Lloyds Pharmacy, and many others let you manage prescriptions and order medicines for delivery.
  • Blood pressure / health monitors — many home health devices connect to phone apps to track readings over time, which you can share with your GP.
💊

Repeat Prescriptions Made Easy

The NHS App lets you order repeat prescriptions online — no more calling the surgery and waiting on hold. You select what you need, and it's sent to your nominated pharmacy, ready to collect when it suits you.

🎬 Entertainment on Demand

One of the great joys of a tablet or smart TV is having virtually unlimited entertainment available whenever you want it. Here's a quick guide to the main options:

  • YouTube — free, enormous library of videos on every topic imaginable: cooking, gardening, travel, music, history, comedy. Subscribe to channels you love and they appear in your "feed."
  • BBC iPlayer — watch BBC TV programmes live or catch up for free. Includes archive programmes. Free with a TV licence.
  • ITV X / Channel 4 / My5 — similar catch-up services for ITV, Channel 4, and Channel 5. All free.
  • Netflix / Disney+ — subscription streaming services (monthly fee) with thousands of films and series. Download and watch offline too.
  • Spotify / Apple Music — stream millions of songs, podcasts, and audiobooks. Spotify has a free tier with adverts.
  • Audible — audiobooks. Ideal if your eyesight makes reading difficult. Paid subscription, but many books are available through your local library app (BorrowBox) for free.

🛒 Shopping Online

Online shopping can save time, effort, and sometimes money. The main places to shop online include:

  • Amazon — vast range, fast delivery, easy returns
  • Supermarket websites — Tesco, Sainsbury's, Waitrose, M&S all offer home delivery. No more heavy bags to carry.
  • eBay — good for finding second-hand bargains and unique items
  • High street retailers online — John Lewis, Boots, Marks & Spencer, Next — most have excellent websites

Remember the safety tips from Module 6: always check for the padlock symbol, use a credit card where possible, and stick to trusted retailers.

Key Takeaways

  • ✓ Apps are programs for your phone or tablet — most are free and easy to install from the app store
  • ✓ Maps apps (Google Maps, Apple Maps) give turn-by-turn directions and help you explore confidently
  • ✓ Online banking is safe and convenient — always access it through the official app or website only
  • ✓ The NHS App lets you book appointments and order prescriptions from home
  • ✓ YouTube, BBC iPlayer, and streaming services put entertainment at your fingertips
