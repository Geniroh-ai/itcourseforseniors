You Already Know More Than You Think

Before we begin, here's something important to understand: you are not starting from zero. Over your lifetime, you've learned countless complex skills — driving a car, cooking recipes, managing a household, perhaps even running a business. Technology is just another skill, and it's one that anyone can learn at any age.

Many people in their 60s, 70s, and even 80s use computers, smartphones, and even AI tools every single day. Not because they were born "tech people" — but because they decided to give it a proper try, just like you're doing now.

💡

A Gentle Reminder

It's completely normal to feel a little unsure at first. Every expert was once a beginner. Be patient with yourself, and celebrate every small win.

Why Does This Matter for You?

You might wonder: "Why should I bother?" Here are some very real benefits that this course will help you access:

1

Stay Connected to Family

Video call your grandchildren, share photos, send messages — no matter how far away they live.

2

Access Information Instantly

Look up a recipe, check the weather, find a doctor, or research a topic — all in seconds.

3

Manage Everyday Tasks Easily

Online banking, booking appointments, shopping, and paying bills — all from your armchair.

4

Use Powerful AI Tools

Get a helpful AI assistant to answer questions, write letters, or explain complex topics in plain English.

5

Stay Sharp and Engaged

Learning new skills keeps your mind active and opens up new social opportunities and hobbies.

How This Course Is Designed for You

This course was built specifically with you in mind. We've thought carefully about what works for adult learners coming to technology for the first time (or returning after a gap). Here's what you can expect:

  • Plain English only — no confusing jargon, or if a technical word is used, it's explained immediately
  • Real-life analogies — we'll compare technology to things you already know and use
  • No pressure — there are no deadlines, no grades, and no one watching. Go at your own pace
  • Short, focused modules — each one takes 10–15 minutes, with a short quiz to reinforce your learning
  • Gradual build-up — we start very simply and gradually introduce more interesting ideas as your confidence grows
🏡

Think of It Like Learning to Drive

When you first learned to drive, someone didn't throw you on a motorway. You started in a car park, then quiet streets, then busier roads. This course works the same way — we build confidence step by step before tackling the exciting bits.

Tips for Learning Success

  • Read slowly — there's no rush. Re-read any section that doesn't feel clear
  • Try the quizzes — even if you get answers wrong, reading the explanation teaches you something
  • Take notes if you like — a simple notebook beside you can be very helpful
  • Do one module at a time — spacing out your learning helps it stick in your memory
  • Ask for help — if someone nearby uses technology, don't hesitate to ask them to demonstrate something
